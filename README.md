## Install

1. Download the zip above.
2. Right-click it > **Extract All** (for example to Documents).
3. Open the folder and double-click **FormPilot.exe**. (Tip: right-click it > Send to > Desktop to make a shortcut.)
4. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** > **Run anyway**.
5. Sign in with your email. Your first **5 forms are free**.

FormPilot uses Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (already on Windows) to fill the forms. Your data stays on your PC.

## How it works

1. Drop in your Excel file
2. Paste the link of your form
3. Columns are matched to the form fields automatically
4. See how long it will take, click **Start**, and get a full report of every row
